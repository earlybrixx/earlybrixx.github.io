This privacy policy applies exclusively to the app Cubie Twist (hereinafter "the App"), developed and published by Holger Buddenberg.

2. General Principle

Cubie Twist is a puzzle game. The core game itself works fully offline and does not transmit any personal data. However, unlike some of the developer's other apps, Dice Journey shows advertising (Google AdMob) and offers an optional in-app purchase ("Remove Ads") processed through Google Play. Both features involve data processing by Google, as described in sections 4 and 5 below.

3. Locally Stored Data

The App stores game-related data locally on the user's device, such as level progress, star ratings, language preference, and sound settings. This data never leaves the device and is not linked to any individual person.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays advertising provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To serve and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address, and general app usage information. This data is processed by Google, not by the developer.

Where required by law (e.g. for users in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland), the App shows a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) before any personalized advertising is shown, allowing you to choose whether ads may be personalized. You can review or withdraw this consent at any time via the same form, which reappears if you clear the App's data.

You can also limit ad personalization directly on your device under Android Settings → Google → Ads (e.g. "Opt out of Ads Personalization" or "Delete advertising ID", depending on Android version). For details on how Google processes this data, see Google's Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy) and "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

5. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The App offers an optional in-app purchase ("Remove Ads"). Purchases are processed entirely through Google Play's billing system. The developer does not receive or store any payment details (such as credit card information); only confirmation that a purchase was completed is received, in order to unlock the corresponding feature. For details on how Google processes payment data, see Google Play's Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy linked above.

6. Unity Engine

The App was built with the Unity engine. Unity Technologies may collect limited technical device data as part of the engine's licensing and operations. The developer has no influence over this processing. For more information, please refer to Unity's privacy policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

7. Data Recipients

Data described in sections 4 and 5 is processed by Google (Google AdMob / Google Play) as an independent data controller for its own purposes (ad serving/measurement and payment processing). No other third parties receive data from the App, and the App contains no separate analytics or tracking services beyond what is described above.

8. User Rights

The developer does not directly collect or store personal data, so there is generally no need to exercise rights of access, rectification, or erasure against the developer. For data processed by Google (advertising, purchases), you can exercise your rights directly with Google via myaccount.google.com. If you have any questions regarding data protection, you may also contact the controller listed above at any time by e-mail.

9. Right to Lodge a Complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The competent authority in North Rhine-Westphalia, Germany, is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (LDI NRW):
www.ldi.nrw.de

Zur Ausspielung und Messung von Werbung können Google und dessen Werbepartner Geräte-Kennungen (z.B. die Android-Werbe-ID), die IP-Adresse sowie allgemeine App-Nutzungsdaten erheben. Diese Einwilligung können Sie jederzeit über dasselbe Formular überprüfen oder widerrufen; es erscheint erneut, wenn Sie die App-Daten löschen.

Zusätzlich lässt sich personalisierte Werbung direkt in den Geräteeinstellungen einschränken, unter Android-Einstellungen → Google → Werbung (z.B. "Werbepersonalisierung deaktivieren" oder "Werbe-ID löschen", je nach Android-Version). Details zur Datenverarbeitung durch Google finden sich in Googles Datenschutzerklärung (policies.google.com/privacy) sowie unter "Wie Google Daten von Websites oder Apps verwendet, die unsere Dienste nutzen" (policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
