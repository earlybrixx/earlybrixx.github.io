Privacy Policy – Who needs numbers?

This privacy policy applies exclusively to the app Who needs numbers? (hereinafter "the App"), developed and published by Holger Buddenberg.

1. Controller

Holger Buddenberg
E-Mail: earlybrixx@gmail.com

2. General Principle

Who needs numbers? is a puzzle game that is free to play and financed by advertising. The game itself works offline. The developer does not operate any servers and does not collect personal data. When the device is online, ads are delivered by Google AdMob (see section 5).

3. Locally Stored Data

The App stores only game-related data locally on the user's device, such as game progress or settings. This data never leaves the device and is not linked to any individual person.

4. Unity Engine

The App was built with the Unity engine. Unity Technologies may collect limited technical device data as part of the engine's licensing and operations. The developer has no influence over this processing. For more information, please refer to Unity's privacy policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays ads using Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland ("Google"). Google may process the device's advertising ID, IP address, device and operating system information, language settings, approximate location derived from the IP address, and information about interactions with ads. This is used to deliver and measure ads, to prevent fraud and – if you have consented – to personalise ads.

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for their consent via Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) when they first launch the App. The legal basis is your consent pursuant to Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and Section 25(1) TDDDG. Without consent, only non-personalised or limited ads may be shown. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time via the "Privacy" button in the App's main menu.

Google may transfer data to countries outside the EU, in particular the USA. Google is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. More information: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in the Android settings (Google → Ads).

Users who purchased the App before it became free to play do not see any ads.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13.

7. User Rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to withdraw your consent at any time (Art. 7(3), Art. 15–21 GDPR). As the developer does not store any personal data itself, please contact Google directly regarding data processed by Google. If you have any questions regarding data protection, you may contact the controller listed above at any time by e-mail.

8. Right to Lodge a Complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The competent authority in North Rhine-Westphalia, Germany, is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (LDI NRW):
www.ldi.nrw.de
