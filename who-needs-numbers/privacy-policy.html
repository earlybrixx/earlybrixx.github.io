2. General Principle

Who needs numbers? is a local puzzle game that works entirely offline. No personal data is collected, stored, or processed by the developer. No data is transmitted to external servers.

3. Locally Stored Data

The App stores only game-related data locally on the user's device, such as game progress or settings. This data never leaves the device and is not linked to any individual person.

4. Unity Engine

The App was built with the Unity engine. Unity Technologies may collect limited technical device data as part of the engine's licensing and operations. The developer has no influence over this processing. For more information, please refer to Unity's privacy policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

5. No Third-Party Sharing

No data is shared with third parties. The App contains no advertising, no tracking, and no analytics services.

6. User Rights

Since no personal data is collected, there is generally no need to exercise rights of access, rectification, or erasure. If you have any questions regarding data protection, you may contact the controller listed above at any time by e-mail.

7. Right to Lodge a Complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The competent authority in North Rhine-Westphalia, Germany, is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (LDI NRW):
